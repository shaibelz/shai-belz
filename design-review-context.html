The Constraint — What We Landed On

SEO required a notification-based approach

To avoid cloaking risk, the SEO team required us not to auto-switch on page load. Instead, the page loads in the merchant's currency, then after ~5 seconds a notification appears and switches to the buyer's local currency.

Why this concerns us
🔴
Trust erosion
The notification reveals the site isn't local — "we see you're from the US" tells the buyer this is a foreign site. Creates hesitation and undermines confidence.
⏱
5-second bounce risk
Buyer sees prices in ₪ for the first 5 seconds. Many will bounce before the switch even happens — especially on mobile where attention spans are shortest.

Our Proposal

Start with real auto-display.
Monitor. Roll back if needed.

Auto display — instantly showing the buyer's local currency — is the standard at Shopify and across eCommerce. The notification approach weakens the very experience we're trying to improve.

🛒
Industry standard
Shopify auto-displays local currency by default for cross-border buyers. Nike, Apple, and others do the same with no option to switch. We're more flexible — buyers can revert via the converter.
📊
Zero SEO impact observed
We ran an experiment on Multi Currency merchants where <20% of traffic comes from SEO. Zero impact on organic traffic was detected. SEO team warns it could take up to 6 months to appear — but we have a mitigation plan.
⚖️
The alternative is worse
Without auto-display, buyers see foreign currency with no way to change it. That's the current experience for 95% of cross-border visits. The notification approach is better than nothing — but it introduces new problems that real auto-display avoids entirely.
✓
Proposed approach
Roll out real auto-display to eligible merchants. Monitor SEO continuously, site by site. If any impact is detected — roll back immediately for that site. Gradual rollout gives us control at every step.
